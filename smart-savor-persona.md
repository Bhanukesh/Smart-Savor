# Smart Savor — Personas & Jobs-to-be-Done

*Working name · 2026-06-27*

---

## Primary Persona — "Maria," the Private-Practice Dietitian

**Who she is**
- Registered Dietitian, runs a small private practice (or works inside a chronic-care program). Caseload of 30–80 patients, many with diabetes, hypertension, CKD, or on GLP-1 follow-up.
- Cash-pay or program-funded. Time-poor; sees patients in 30–45 min slots, weeks apart.
- Comfortable with charting tools (Healthie/Nutrium) but frustrated they show *plans*, not *behavior*.

**Her reality today**
- She knows the right advice and gives it. Patients nod, leave, and **don't follow it**. She finds out only at the next visit — if they show up.
- She has no objective read on what the patient actually buys or eats, and no way to demonstrate her work moved the numbers.

**The job she's hiring Smart Savor to do**
> *"When a chronic patient leaves my office, help me **see whether they're actually following the plan** and **prove the diet is working** — so I spend less time guessing and can show real results."*

**Sub-jobs**
1. *Translate a flagged gap into swaps the patient will realistically buy* (affordable, close to current habits) — without building plans from scratch.
2. *Stay in control* — approve or adjust every recommendation; nothing clinical goes out without her sign-off.
3. *See behavior between visits*, not just at the next appointment.
4. *Prove outcomes* with lab improvement she can show patients, programs, and eventually payers.

**What makes her switch / adopt**
- It surfaces the "aha" instantly: *this patient keeps buying X; for the same money, Y closes the iron gap.*
- It plugs into how patients already shop (receipts), not another logging chore she has to police.
- Free to start; proves itself on her own patients.

**What makes her churn**
- Fabricated or noisy data she can't trust. Daily-logging dependence. Recommendations over budget or wildly off the patient's habits.

## Secondary Persona — "Sam," the Chronic-Care Patient (beneficiary, not buyer)

**Who he is**
- Has a diet-driven chronic condition. Cost-sensitive, busy, not a quantified-self enthusiast. Has tried a logging app and quit.

**The job he's hiring Smart Savor to do**
> *"Help me eat better for my condition **without changing my whole life or budget** — tell me the small, cheap swaps that matter and don't make me log every bite."*

**Sub-jobs**
1. *Get credit for what I already do* — upload my last few months of receipts instead of starting from zero.
2. *Pick foods I'll actually eat* to close a gap.
3. *Log easily when I want to* — photo or voice, not forms.

**What he needs to feel**
- That the effort is minimal and the plan respects his budget and tastes.
- That his dietitian — someone he trusts — is behind the recommendations.

---

## Anti-Persona — Who this is NOT for (v1)
- **The self-directed consumer** with no clinician. (No accountability loop, no labs → adherence collapses, no payer.)
- **The acute/hospital clinical setting** requiring certified diagnostic tooling. (Smart Savor is a workflow tool; clinical authority stays with the practitioner.)
