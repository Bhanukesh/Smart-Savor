Product walkthrough

The Smart Savor flow, screen by screen

Your doctor tells your body what it needs — you decide what's on your plate. Follow the five stages of the loop. The ⭐ screen is the USP moment.

Dietitian · D1.5

1 · Prioritize the cycle

The agent drafts a 6–10 focus set from Sam's labs, with reasons, pairs & conflicts. Maria confirms or overrides — she stays in clinical control.

 Dietitian · D2/D3

2 · Ratify the safe menu

The agent drafts a comorbidity-screened swap menu per gap. Maria approves / edits / removes — so every option Sam can pick is safe for him.

 Patient · P2 · ⭐ THE USP

3 · Sam chooses his food

Choose → recompute → still-approved. Same iron target, a food Sam actually likes — the app just tells him how much. Interactive: pick a food.

 Patient · P4

4 · Track progress (honestly)

Targets trending toward "in range" — driven by what Sam actually logged, labeled as intake, never a blood level.

 Patient · P5 · future

5 · Fresh Box out of scope v1

The signature differentiator: auto-order the week's approved fresh produce to Sam's door. "We don't just tell you what to eat — we put it on your doorstep."
Between visits

The weekly nudge

Bought spinach but never logged it? One gentle weekly nudge — never daily. See it on the dashboard screen.
