# Smart Savor — Product Brief (One-Pager)

*2026-06-27*

---

## The Problem
Dietitians serving chronic-care patients don't struggle to *prescribe* the right diet — that advice is near-commodity. They struggle to know whether the patient **actually follows it**, and to **prove it worked**. Between appointments they're blind: they set targets and hope. With ~80% of healthcare spend tied to diet-related chronic disease, that blind spot means unmanaged conditions, wasted visits, and no evidence of impact.

## Who It's For
**Dietitians, nutritionists, and health coaches** managing patients with diet-driven chronic conditions.
- **Market:** chronic-care programs (diabetes, CKD, hypertension, GLP-1 follow-up) — where outcomes are measured and payers reimburse.
- **Beachhead:** private-practice dietitians serving chronic patients — fast to deploy, cash-rich, no procurement committee.
- The **patient** is the beneficiary and a data-entry surface; the **dietitian** is the user and stays in clinical control.

## The Core Bet
That the winning product is not a nutrient calculator but an **adherence-and-outcomes instrument** (Smart Savor), built on one hard-to-copy mechanism:

> **Ingest a patient's real grocery receipts → model what they actually eat → recommend gap-closing, low-disruption swaps the dietitian approves → validate with a 3-month lab.**

Swaps are ranked **primarily by how fast they close the gap and how little they disrupt current habits**; **affordability is an optional signal** held in reserve for a future B2C launch (cost stays in the data model, not enforced in B2B v1). The wedge is **receipt-to-habit ingestion**. The durable moat is the **outcome dataset** that loop generates — which interventions, for which gaps, produced real lab improvement. Incumbents (Healthie, Nutrium, Cronometer) own the workflow but don't run this loop and can't retroactively manufacture the data. That data is what unlocks **white-labeling and insurance reimbursement**.

## What Success Looks Like
- **North Star:** ≥**70% of patients show measurable lab improvement at 3-month re-test** (vs. a defined baseline).
- **Leading:** ≥80% of patients get a usable eating-habit model from uploaded receipts; ≥70% swap approve/adjust rate; ≥85% of gaps have a viable gap-closing swap.
- **Business:** free MVP proves the loop on real patients → white-label licensing to programs → insurance reimbursement codes funded by proven outcomes.
